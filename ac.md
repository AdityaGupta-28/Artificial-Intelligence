### The main idea behind this innovation is to make traditional prescription glasses intelligent and adaptive.
In conventional prescription glasses, the lens power is generally fixed based on the prescription determined during an eye examination. However, our proposed innovation aims to make the lens power adjustable according to the required vision correction.
The system consists of three major components:
1. **AI-based Processing Unit:** It processes the collected vision-related information and determines the required lens correction.
2. **Adaptive Lens:** It is the component whose optical power can be adjusted according to the correction requirement.
3. **Control System:** It acts as an interface between the AI processing unit and the adaptive lens. It receives the adjustment requirements and controls the lens accordingly.

The working process is quite straightforward. First, the system collects relevant vision information. Then, the AI-based processing unit analyzes that information and determines the required correction. Finally, the control system sends the appropriate commands to the adaptive lens to adjust its optical power.
The main objective of this innovation is to develop intelligent prescription glasses that provide adaptive vision correction rather than relying entirely on conventional fixed-power lenses.
