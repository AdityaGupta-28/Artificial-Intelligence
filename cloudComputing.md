Cloud computing is the delivery of computing resources (servers, storage, databases, networking, software, analytics) over the internet, on demand, instead of owning and maintaining physical hardware yourself. You rent what you need from a provider like AWS, Microsoft Azure, or Google Cloud and pay only for what you use.

### Service models

IaaS (Infrastructure as a Service): raw virtual machines, storage, and networks. You manage the OS and everything above it. Example: AWS EC2.
PaaS (Platform as a Service): a ready platform to deploy code without managing servers. Example: Google App Engine, Heroku.
SaaS (Software as a Service): finished applications used through a browser. Example: Gmail, Google Docs.

## Deployment models
Public cloud: shared infrastructure run by a provider.
Private cloud: dedicated to a single organization.
Hybrid cloud: a mix of public and private.
